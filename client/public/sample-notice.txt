DELHI TECHNOLOGICAL UNIVERSITY
OFFICE OF THE DEAN, STUDENT WELFARE

NOTICE: MERIT-CUM-MEANS STUDENT SUPPORT SCHOLARSHIP 2026-27
Notice Reference: DSW/SCH/2026/041
Date of Issue: 20 October 2026

Applications are invited from eligible undergraduate students of Delhi Technological University for the Merit-cum-Means Student Support Scholarship for the academic session 2026-27.

ELIGIBILITY
1. The applicant must be a currently enrolled full-time undergraduate student of the University.
2. The annual family income from all sources must not exceed INR 5,00,000.
3. The applicant must have passed the previous academic year without any active academic backlog.
4. Students receiving another full tuition-fee scholarship from a government or private source are not eligible for this particular award. Students receiving partial assistance should disclose it in the application for review.

SCHOLARSHIP BENEFIT
Selected students may receive financial assistance of up to INR 40,000 for the academic session. The final amount will be determined by the scholarship committee based on verified need and available funds.

DOCUMENTS TO SUBMIT
- Completed scholarship application form.
- Income certificate issued by a competent government authority, valid for the current financial year.
- Previous academic year's marksheet or official grade card.
- Copy of the current university identity card.
- First page of the applicant's bank passbook showing name and account details. Applicants should mask account information not required for verification.
- Self-declaration of any other scholarship or financial assistance received.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Download the application form from the Student Welfare section of the University website.
2. Complete all fields and attach self-attested copies of the required documents.
3. Submit the application to the Student Welfare Office, Administrative Block, Room 214.
4. Write “MCM Scholarship 2026-27” on the top of the envelope.

IMPORTANT DATES
- Notice issued: 20 October 2026.
- Last date for submission: 15 November 2026, by 4:00 PM.
- Incomplete applications and applications received after the deadline will not be considered.
- Shortlisted applicants may be contacted for document verification after the submission deadline.

CONTACT
For queries, contact the Student Welfare Office at scholarships@dtu.ac.in or visit Room 214, Administrative Block, on working days between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
